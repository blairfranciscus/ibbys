Welcome

Legacy in action

The Danforth Scholars Program supports students known for leadership, character, and service.

Danforth Scholars
The Danforth family played a foundational role in shaping Washington University in St. Louis and the surrounding community. Their legacy extends beyond campus through leadership in education, service, and innovation—including the founding of the Purina company, which helped establish St. Louis as a center for industry and philanthropy.
